# Meal planner

A family plans meals, turns them into a shopping list, and walks a chosen store.

## Language

**Basisvare**:
An ingredient the family usually has at home, so it is held off the shopping list until someone puts it back on the trip.
_Avoid_: stock ingredient, pantry item, staple

**Ingredient category**:
The classification of a shopping item, such as dairy or dry goods. It is the same no matter which store the family is shopping in.
_Avoid_: section, aisle

**Section**:
One ingredient category as a stop in a particular store, with that store's name and walk order for it. Store mode shows each section as a heading.
_Avoid_: aisle, category, empty category

**Store order**:
The sequence of sections in the selected store, the order you walk that store.
_Avoid_: sort order

**Store mode**:
The shopping screen for walking the selected store on one trip.
_Avoid_: shopping list

**Trip**:
The items store mode is showing for the chosen focus: this week, next week, or every open week.
_Avoid_: week, list

**Open section**:
A section that still has at least one item left to buy on the current trip.
_Avoid_: open category, lifted category

**Finished section**:
A section whose items on the current trip are all checked.
_Avoid_: empty category, empty section

**Tag**:
A word on a recipe, such as middag or rask. The same word on a family recipe and a standard recipe is the same tag.
_Avoid_: keyword, category, label
